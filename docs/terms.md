## Forensic findings are observations, not conclusions

The library reports *observed facts* and *"consistent with"* inferences (the `Anomaly` findings and provenance summary). It deliberately does not assert intent, tampering, or guilt — those determinations are for the examiner and the tribunal. Findings are heuristics over the on-disc structures and may have benign explanations; treat them as leads to corroborate, not verdicts.
